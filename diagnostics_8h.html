◆ diagnostics_periodic()

void diagnostics_periodic ( const diagnostics_can_fns_t * can_fns)

Periodically samples task statistics and transmits diagnostics over CAN.

Samples all tasks once per second (every 20 ticks at 50 ms) Sends at most one task (status + name character) per tick and one board summary per cycle.
