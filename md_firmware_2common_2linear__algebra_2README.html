Linear algebra

Linear algebra with no fancy optimizations or frivolous features.

vec3_t provides:

  • addition
  • subtraction
  • scaling
  • magnitude
  • normalization

mat3_t provides:

  • addition
  • subtraction
  • scaling
  • matrix and vector multiplication
  • column extraction
  • outer product
  • symmetrization
  • Cholesky factorization
